‌Profit Calculator: Support Document & User Guide

Welcome to the official Profit Calculator Support Document and User Guide. Below, you will find comprehensive instructions on how to use all core features, perform multi-step financial calculations, export or print your digital paper tape logs, and resolve any operational issues.

‌📖 User Guide

‌Overview

Profit Calculator is a standalone financial utility designed to deliver fast, step-by-step evaluations for business pricing, trade management, and investment growth. Featuring a retro LCD paper tape interface, the app guides you through multi-prompt inputs, parses inline arithmetic formulas, and allows you to print or copy calculation logs effortlessly.


‌Key Calculation Modes & How to Use Them

To select a mode, tap any of the mode buttons at the top of the screen. 1

Basic Math Mode (0 + ENTER)

Universal Math


Type 0 on the keypad and press ENTER from the welcome screen, or type any formula (e.g., 55

- 5 or 1200 * 1.05) directly into prompt fields. Verification: The LCD screen will display [In Basic Calc Mode] and parse entries sequentially.


2

% Margin & Markup Mode Pricing


Tap % Margin. When prompted, enter your Cost, press ENTER, then enter your Sell Price, and press ENTER. Verification: The paper tape displays the dollar Profit, Margin %,

and Markup %.


3

Stop Loss & Take Profit Mode Trading


Tap Stop Loss. Enter your Asset Price, Stop Loss %, and Take Profit %, pressing ENTER after each step. Verification: The app outputs the exact Stop Loss Price and Take Profit Price target thresholds.


4

ROIC (Return on Invested Capital)

Capital Efficiency


Tap ROIC. Sequentially input EBIT, Tax Rate (%), Debt, and Equity. Verification: The paper tape displays NOPAT, Invested Capital, and the final ROIC %.


5

CAGR & Annualized ROI Investment Growth


Select CAGR (input Beg Value, End Value, Years) or Annualized ROI (input Initial Inv, Net FV, Years). Verification:The tape outputs the compound annual growth rate or annualized return percentage.


6

Sale Price & % Discount Promotions


Tap Sale Price (enter Original Price and Discount %) or % Discount (enter Original

Price and Sale Price).Verification: View final discounted pricing and customer dollar savings.


‌Viewing, Printing, & Exporting History Tape

  • Persistent Scroll: The green LCD tape automatically records all completed calculation chains across mode changes.

  • Copy to Clipboard: Tap the Copy button to copy formatted text logs directly into notes or messages.

  • AirPrint: Tap the Printer icon to open the native iOS AirPrint dialogue and print your calculation logs.

    ‌🛠️ Support & Troubleshooting

    ‌Frequently Asked Questions


    Issue / Question

    Resolution

    Is an account or sign-in required?

    No. Profit Calculator is a 100% standalone, on-device app with no account creation required.

    Can I type equations inside a prompt?

    Yes! You can enter inline expressions like 100 + 25 or 500 *

    0.2 into any step field.

    How do I clear the tape screen?

    Tap Clear All (or RESET) to reset the calculation state and clear the tape history.

    Where is my calculation data stored?

    All inputs and tape histories are processed in local memory (RAM) and are never sent to remote servers.
