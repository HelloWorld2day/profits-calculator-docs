‌Privacy Policy for Profit Calculator

Effective Date: November 5, 2026


This Privacy Policy describes how Profit Calculator ("we," "our," or "the app") handles user information. We are committed to maintaining the complete privacy and security of your personal data.


  1. ‌No Personal Data Collection

    Profit Calculator operates 100% on-device as a standalone financial utility.


    • We do not collect, capture, log, store, or transmit any personal information, device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or usage metrics.

    • The app does not require user registration, account creation, or login credentials to access any feature.


  2. ‌Calculation Data and Local Processing

    All inputs, mathematical operations, calculation tape histories, and mode states are processed strictly within your device's memory (RAM) and local storage.


    • Your calculation entries, financial formulas, and custom math operations are never saved to external servers, remote databases, or cloud services.

    • Any data exported via AirPrint or copied to the system clipboard is handled locally by native iOS system frameworks (UIKit / UIPasteboard) and remains under your direct control.


  3. ‌Third-Party Services and Analytics

    • No Analytics or Trackers: Profit Calculator does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash report servers, or telemetry tools.

    • No External APIs: The app does not connect to external data providers, payment gateways, authentication providers, or remote AI processing APIs.


  4. ‌Data Sharing and Sales

    Because Profit Calculator does not collect or store any user data or financial history, we do not sell, rent, trade, share, or disclose any personal or financial information to third parties under any circumstances.


  5. ‌Children's Privacy

    Because the app collects no personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards. No data is collected from children or any other demographic.


  6. ‌Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future app enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
